Thrust I

Research / Thrust I

Discovering physics from data

Governing equations are rarely known for real systems. This thrust learns them from sparse, noisy, output-only measurements by combining stochastic calculus, sparse learning and Bayesian inference.

Methods and developments

Stochastic systems discovery

Discovers the interpretable drift and diffusion terms of nonlinear stochastic dynamical systems driven by Gaussian white noise, using sparse Bayesian learning on output-only measurements.

Paper
  • MSSP 2023
Code
  • Output-only physics learning

Lagrangian discovery

Discovers the interpretable Lagrangian of a dynamical system directly from data, with a Bayesian version that reports uncertainty on the identified terms.

Papers
  • CPC 2024
  • Bayesian version, MSSP 2024
Code
  • Lagrangian discovery
  • Bayesian Lagrangian

SPDE discovery

Discovers stochastic partial differential equations from limited data using variational Bayes inference, building on a Bayesian framework for learning deterministic PDEs.

Papers
  • SPDEs, CMAME 2024
  • PDEs, Physica D 2023

Stochastic Lagrangian discovery

Discovers the Lagrangian of stochastically excited dynamical systems, including the interpretable diffusion coefficient of the stochastic excitation.

Applications

MAntRA

A framework for model-agnostic reliability analysis: it discovers equations from data, generates the random excitation, and evaluates the reliability of a system.
