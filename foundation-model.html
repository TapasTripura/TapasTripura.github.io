Scientific foundation model

Neural Combinatorial Wavelet Neural Operator (NCWNO)

Building a new machine-learning model from scratch for every scientific task takes a lot of computational time and memory. NCWNO is a foundation model for computational mechanics. It is pre-trained on a diverse spectrum of physics and then adapts to the solution operators of unseen parametric PDEs.

What it does

  • Learns solution operators for several parametric PDEs at once.
  • Generalizes to new parametric PDEs with minimal fine-tuning.

What sets it apart

  • It does not suffer catastrophic forgetting.
  • It keeps positive transfer to new parametric PDEs.
  • It transfers knowledge across dissimilar tasks.

In the showcase experiment, one pre-trained NCWNO predicts six PDEs simultaneously on a 64 × 64 spatial grid.
