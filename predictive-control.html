Research / Thrust III

Predictive control without known physics

Learning effective control policies for systems whose physics is unknown, with high sample efficiency, which matters most where trial-and-error interaction is infeasible.

Methods and developments

MAP-TD

A deep reinforcement learning method for controlling systems whose physics is unknown. A parsimonious dynamics model, discovered offline with sparse Bayesian learning, predicts long-horizon trajectories inside the agent, which improves sample efficiency and control quality. Demonstrations include deflection control of a cantilever beam, wind-induced vibration control of a 76-degree-of-freedom structure, and active flow control around a circular cylinder, with zero-shot transfer to a square cylinder.
