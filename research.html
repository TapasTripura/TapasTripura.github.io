Research

My research is organized into three thrusts. Each one has methods and developments, and applications.

  1. Discovering physics from data
  2. Neural operators for computational mechanics
  3. Predictive control without known physics

Thrust I

Discovering physics from data

Governing equations are rarely known for real systems. This thrust learns them from sparse, noisy, output-only measurements by combining stochastic calculus, sparse learning and Bayesian inference.

Thrust II

Neural operators for computational mechanics

Neural operators learn the map from the parameters of a PDE to its solution. Wavelets are localized in both space and frequency, so wavelet neural operators track fine patterns in how solutions depend on the parameters.
